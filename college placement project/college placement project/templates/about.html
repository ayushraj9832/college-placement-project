About PlaceHub

A single source of truth for campus recruitment

PlaceHub is the College Placement and Recruitment Management System used by students, recruiters, placement officers and college administration. It replaces scattered spreadsheets and email threads with one database-backed workflow.

The problem

Placement cells usually manage hundreds of students, dozens of companies and multiple recruitment rounds using spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and email. This creates duplicate records, missed deadlines, inconsistent eligibility checks and no reliable way to answer simple questions such as “how many MCA students are still unplaced?”

Recruiters cannot see a verified applicant list, students do not know why they are ineligible, and administrators cannot produce accurate placement statistics without manually combining files.

Our solution

A relational MySQL database stores students, companies, jobs, applications, rounds, interviews, offers and reports. A Flask application exposes four role-based dashboards on top of that single database, so every user sees the same accurate information with the permissions appropriate to their role.

Objectives

What the system is designed to achieve

Centralise records

Keep student academic data, company profiles, jobs, applications and offers in one consistent database.

Automate eligibility

Compare CGPA and branch against each job’s criteria before a student applies, removing manual screening errors.

Track recruitment

Follow every application through rounds, interview results, selection and final offer status.

Measure outcomes

Calculate placement percentage, average and highest packages, and branch/company performance from live data.

Enforce integrity

Use primary keys, foreign keys, unique constraints, checks and triggers to keep the data trustworthy.

Support decisions

Give the placement cell and administration the reports they need for reviews, accreditation and planning.
