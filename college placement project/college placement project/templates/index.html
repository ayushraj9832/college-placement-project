One platform, four roles

Built around the way placements actually work

Every role gets a purpose-built dashboard, and every number comes from the MySQL database.

Students

Build a profile, check eligibility automatically, apply to jobs and track every interview and offer in one timeline.

Recruiters

Publish roles, define CGPA and branch criteria, shortlist applicants, schedule rounds and release offers.

Placement cell

Approve companies, maintain student records, monitor recruitment activity and generate placement reports.

Administration

College-wide placement percentage, salary statistics, branch and company performance, and officer oversight.

Secure by design

Hashed passwords, session-based login, role-based access control and parameterised SQL throughout.

Real DBMS work

Nine normalised tables, ten foreign keys, views, stored procedures, functions, triggers and transactions.

How it works

From registration to offer letter

A transparent four-step flow for every student.

Create your profile

Add branch, batch year, CGPA and contact details. Your profile powers every eligibility check.

Find eligible roles

Browse verified openings and instantly see whether you meet the CGPA and branch criteria.

Apply and interview

Submit applications, track shortlisting and receive interview schedules with round details.

Accept your offer

View final selection results, package details and joining date in your placement dashboard.
